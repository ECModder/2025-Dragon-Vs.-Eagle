# ============================================================
#   DvE - dissolving the government and forming a new one.
#   Players only, and only in countries that hold elections.
#   1. Dissolve  2. task a party with at least 20%  3. invite
#   partners from its side  4. form the government with >50%.
#   No party at 20%, or a failed coalition: random elections.
#   Rules: coalition lists and incompatibilities in
#   DVE_government_triggers.txt / the invite decisions below.
# ============================================================
