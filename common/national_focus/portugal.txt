#   replaces the 1936 tree (as romania.txt / poland.txt do).
#
#   LAYOUT (x columns)
#      0 - 12   ECONOMY
#     14 - 44   OPENING and the three presidential routes
#               (Montenegro 14-24, Santos 26-34, Ventura 36-44)
#     48 - 65   CRISTIANO RONALDO (hidden until 5 November 2026)
#     68 - 92   STRATEGIC DEFENCE REVIEW: Army 68-74, Air 77-83, Navy 86-92
#
#   ROUTES
#   The player backs a candidate with one of three mutually exclusive
#   focuses before the election of 18 January 2026. The route flag
#   (POR_route_*) is set at the inauguration of 9 March 2026 by
#   POR_inaugurate_winner. Every candidate focus checks its route flag,
#   so nothing of an old route can be taken after Ronaldo takes office.
#
#   Dates are scheduled in common/scripted_effects/POR_effects.txt.
# ============================================================
